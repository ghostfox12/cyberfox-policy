Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

CyberFox Labs ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile applications collect, use, and protect your information.

Our Applications

Foxalyst

Information We Collect

Foxalyst does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers — because we do not operate any servers. The app has no backend, no analytics, and no account system.

All data you enter into the app — village names, Town Hall/Builder Hall levels, building and troop levels, and any village data you paste in from a Clash of Clans game export — is stored locally on your device only, inside the app's local database.

Data Storage

All user data is stored locally on your device using an on-device SQLite database. We do not operate external servers, databases, or cloud storage. Your data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all associated data.

Clash of Clans Data

Foxalyst lets you paste a JSON export of your Clash of Clans village (obtained directly from the game) to track your building, troop, and hero upgrade progress. This data is parsed and stored locally on your device and is never sent anywhere. Foxalyst is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Supercell in any way. Clash of Clans is a trademark of Supercell Oy.

Third-Party Services

Foxalyst does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not display ads and has no in-app purchases.

Permissions

Foxalyst requests no permissions at all — not even internet access. The app is fully offline: it makes no network connections of any kind, so no data can leave your device.

Your Rights (GDPR / KVKK)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: All your data is visible within the app at any time — there is no separate copy anywhere else.
  • Right to Deletion: You can delete any village from within the app, or delete all data by uninstalling the app.
  • Right to Portability: Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control and portability over your data.

Legal Basis for Processing: Since Foxalyst does not transmit or process personal data outside your device, no data processing by us occurs that would require a legal basis under GDPR/KVKK.

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and since Foxalyst collects no personal information at all, this is inherently satisfied.

Security

Since all data stays on your device, its security depends on your device's built-in security features (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
