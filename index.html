Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

CyberFox Labs ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile applications and websites collect, use, and protect your information.

Our Applications

Foxalyst

No Account System

Foxalyst does not require registration or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or a password, and we do not store any of these.

Clash of Clans Lookups

Foxalyst lets you look up any Clash of Clans player or clan by tag. When you do this, the tag you enter is sent to Supercell's official Clash of Clans API (api.clashofclans.com) and the result is shown to you immediately. We do not permanently store the tags you search or the results returned — each lookup is a stand-alone request.

The player/clan information shown (trophies, level, clan membership, etc.) is already public within the Clash of Clans game itself; Foxalyst only displays it, it does not generate it.

Legend League Statistics

For players in the Legend League, Foxalyst collects and stores season-long battle data (player tag, army composition used, attack results) that is available through Supercell's public API, in order to compute aggregate "meta army" statistics. This is a compilation of information that is already public within the game; no additional personal profiling is performed.

Data Stored On Your Device

To improve your experience, Foxalyst stores the following locally on your device only, using your browser/app's local storage. This information is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Your recent searches (player/clan tag and name)
  • Players/clans you've added to favorites
  • Your selected theme, color, and language preference

You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Advertising

Foxalyst may display ads via Google AdSense in the future. If enabled, Google may use cookies or similar identifiers to personalize ads. See Google's Advertising Policies for details on how Google handles this data. This page will be updated when/if advertising is activated.

Permissions (Mobile App)

The Foxalyst Android app requests only the INTERNET permission, which is required to fetch player/clan data from Supercell's API. It does not request access to your location, camera, contacts, storage, or any other device permission.

Clash of Clans Data & Trademarks

Foxalyst is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Supercell in any way. Clash of Clans is a trademark of Supercell Oy. See Supercell's Fan Content Policy for more information.

Your Rights (GDPR / KVKK)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: We do not maintain personal profiles about individual users, so there is no stored personal data tied to you to access.
  • Right to Deletion: Locally stored preferences (search history, favorites) can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's/browser's storage.
  • Right to Portability: Since Foxalyst does not maintain user accounts or personal profiles, there is no portable personal data set to transfer.

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed at children under 13. Since Foxalyst does not collect any personal identifying information, this is inherently satisfied regardless of the user's age.

Security

All connections between the app and our services are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Data we retain server-side (see "Legend League Statistics" above) consists only of publicly available in-game information and is not linked to any individual user's browsing behavior.
